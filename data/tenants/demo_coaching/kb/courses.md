# Courses

Meridian Demo Academy teaches for two entrance exams, JEE (Main and Advanced) and NEET. Every course is offered in the classroom at the Noida centre and online.

## JEE courses

### JEE Foundation

For students in class 9 and class 10. It covers school mathematics and science in more depth and introduces JEE-style problems. Classes run on three days a week, two hours each day. A batch has at most 30 students.

### JEE Two-Year Programme

For students entering class 11. It covers the full class 11 and class 12 syllabus for JEE Main and JEE Advanced over two years. Classes run on five days a week, four hours each day. A batch has at most 35 students.

### JEE One-Year Programme

For students entering class 12 who have finished the class 11 syllabus elsewhere. It revises class 11 in the first ten weeks and then covers class 12. Classes run on five days a week, four hours each day.

### JEE Dropper Programme

For students who have passed class 12 and are taking a year to prepare. Classes run on six days a week, five hours each day. A batch has at most 40 students.

## NEET courses

### NEET Two-Year Programme

For students entering class 11. It covers physics, chemistry and biology for NEET over two years. Classes run on five days a week, four hours each day. A batch has at most 35 students.

### NEET Dropper Programme

For students who have passed class 12. Classes run on six days a week, five hours each day.

## Online mode

Every course above has an online batch with live classes at the same hours as the classroom batch. Recordings stay available for 30 days. Online students write the same tests as classroom students and get the same printed study material by courier.

## What we do not offer

We do not teach for CUET, olympiads alone, or board exams alone. We do not offer home tuition or one-to-one coaching.
