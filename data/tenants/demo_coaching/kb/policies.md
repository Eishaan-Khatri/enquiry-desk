# Policies

## Refunds

A student who leaves within 15 days of the batch start date gets the course fee back, less 5,000 for study material. A student who leaves between 16 and 45 days gets 50% of the fee paid for that year. No refund is given after 45 days. The registration fee is never refunded.

A refund request is made in writing at the front desk. It is paid to the same bank account within 21 working days. The desk cannot approve a refund. Only the centre head can.

## Changing batch or mode

A student may move between the classroom batch and the online batch once in a year. Moving from classroom to online does not return the fee difference. Moving from online to classroom means paying the difference.

## Attendance

Parents get a message when a student misses a class. A student below 75% attendance in a month is called in with a parent.

## Missed classes

Classroom students can watch the recording of a missed class for 7 days. Online students have recordings for 30 days.

## Doubt sessions

Doubt sessions run every weekday from 2:30 pm to 3:30 pm at the centre and online. No booking is needed.

## Conduct

Mobile phones stay switched off in class. The academy may ask a student to leave for repeated misconduct, and the refund rules above then apply.
