# Admission

## How to join

1. Book a counselling call or visit the centre.
2. Sit the Meridian Scholarship Test, or ask for direct admission without it.
3. Pay the registration fee and the first instalment.
4. Collect the study material and the batch timetable.

Direct admission is open for every course until a batch is full. Seats in a batch are given in the order that fees are paid.

## Meridian Scholarship Test

The test is free. It is a 90 minute paper with 60 multiple choice questions in mathematics and science at the level of the student's current class. It can be taken at the centre or online.

Test dates for the coming session are Sunday 25 October 2026 and Sunday 8 November 2026, both at 10:00 am. Results are sent by message within five days.

## Scholarship slabs

| Score in the test | Reduction in the course fee |
|---|---|
| 90% and above | 50% |
| 80% to 89% | 30% |
| 70% to 79% | 15% |
| Below 70% | None |

The reduction applies to the course fee and not to the registration fee. Only one scholarship applies to a student. A scholarship continues into the second year if the student scores at least 60% in the first-year final test.

## Documents needed

The last school report card, one photograph, and a copy of the student's identity document. Droppers also bring the class 12 mark sheet.

## Demo classes

A student may attend two demo classes without paying. Ask the counsellor to arrange them.
