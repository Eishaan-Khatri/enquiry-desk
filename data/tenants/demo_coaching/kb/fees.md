# Fees

All fees are in rupees and include 18% GST. Fees are for the session that starts in April 2027 and for the early batches that start in November 2026.

## Classroom fees

| Course | Total fee |
|---|---|
| JEE Foundation, per year | 48,000 |
| JEE Two-Year Programme, both years | 2,60,000 |
| JEE One-Year Programme | 1,45,000 |
| JEE Dropper Programme | 1,35,000 |
| NEET Two-Year Programme, both years | 2,40,000 |
| NEET Dropper Programme | 1,25,000 |

## Online fees

The online batch of any course costs 60% of the classroom fee. For example, the JEE Two-Year Programme online is 1,56,000.

## Registration fee

A registration fee of 2,000 is paid once at admission. It is separate from the course fee and is not refunded.

## Instalments

A one-year course can be paid in two instalments: 60% at admission and 40% within 90 days. A two-year course can be paid in four instalments, one at the start of each half year. Paying a full year at once does not reduce the fee.

## What the fee includes

Classes, printed study material, the test series, doubt sessions and library access. It does not include transport, meals or a hostel.

## Scholarships

Fee reductions are given only through the Meridian Scholarship Test. The desk cannot offer a discount. See the admission document for the scholarship slabs.
