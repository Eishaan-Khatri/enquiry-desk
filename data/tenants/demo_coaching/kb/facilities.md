# Centre and facilities

## Location

Meridian Demo Academy has one centre, in Sector 62, Noida. It is a five minute walk from the Sector 62 metro station. There is no other branch. Students outside Noida join the online batch.

## Hostel

The academy has no hostel of its own. The front desk keeps a list of paying guest accommodation near the centre and shares it on request. The academy does not inspect or recommend any of them.

## Transport

The academy runs no buses. Most students come by metro.

## Library

The library seats 80 students and is open from 8:00 am to 8:30 pm, Monday to Saturday. It is free for enrolled students.

## Study material

Each student gets printed modules for every subject, a set of previous years' papers, and a login for the online test platform. Material for online students is sent by courier within ten days of admission.

## Teachers

Each subject in a batch has one teacher for the whole year. The academy has 14 full-time teachers. The desk does not share teachers' phone numbers.

## Parent meetings

A parent meeting is held once every two months. Parents can also ask for a call with the batch mentor at any time.
