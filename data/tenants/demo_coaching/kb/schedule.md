# Batches and timings

## Batch start dates

| Batch | Starts |
|---|---|
| Early batch, for class 10 students moving to class 11 (JEE and NEET) | Monday 16 November 2026 |
| Main session, all courses | Monday 5 April 2027 |
| Dropper batches, JEE and NEET | Monday 14 June 2027 |

The early batch covers the first four chapters of class 11 before the school year starts. Its students join the Two-Year Programme in April and pay no extra fee for the early weeks.

## Class timings

Classroom batches for school-going students run from 4:00 pm to 8:00 pm on weekdays. Dropper batches run from 8:30 am to 1:30 pm, Monday to Saturday. Foundation batches run from 4:30 pm to 6:30 pm on Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

## Tests

Every batch has a chapter test on alternate Sundays, from 9:00 am to 12:00 noon. A full syllabus test is held once a month from January onwards. Results are shared with parents within three days.

## Centre hours

The Noida centre is open from 8:00 am to 8:30 pm, Monday to Saturday. The front desk is closed on Sunday except on test days.

## Holidays

The centre closes for one week at Diwali and one week at the end of December. Classes missed for public holidays are made up on the following Saturday.
